Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does the digital parking pass work?

After booking a slot on KumbhPark, you receive an E-Pass with a QR code and Slot ID (e.g. P01). When you reach the parking entrance gate, scan your pass at the sensor boom barrier or show it to the attendant for express entry.

2. Can I cancel my reservation if my travel plans change?

Yes! Navigate to "My Bookings" in your dashboard and click "Cancel". The slot will be instantly released for other incoming pilgrims.

3. Are EV charging stations available at Ramkund?

Yes, Zone A (Ramkund) and Zone D (Gangapur Road) feature dedicated EV fast-charging bays marked with ⚡ in the visual slot grid.

4. How do I register as a Parking Provider?

Click on "Provider Portal" from the login page, register your organization details, and start adding your parking zones and managing slots immediately.
